# Sovereign Alpha 4D Scoring Methodology

The 4-Dimensional Scoring System is designed to provide a comprehensive, balanced evaluation of an asset by looking at four independent factors.

## 1. Fundamental Score (30% Weight)
Evaluates core business health, looking at earnings growth, margin expansion, debt ratios, and cash flow generation. 

## 2. Technical Score (30% Weight)
Measures market action and momentum. Key indicators include RSI (Relative Strength Index), MACD, volume spikes, and moving average crossovers. The system uniquely rewards extreme oversold conditions if fundamentals remain intact.

## 3. Sentiment Score (20% Weight)
Calculated via Natural Language Processing (NLP) over alternative data sources including podcast transcripts, executive interviews, and financial news flow.

## 4. Macro Score (20% Weight)
Factors in the broader economic regime (RISK_ON, RISK_OFF, NEUTRAL) determined by FII flows, bond yields, and currency momentum. 

## Composite Score Formula
`Overall = (Fund * 0.3) + (Tech * 0.3) + (Sent * 0.2) + (Macro * 0.2)`

Scores map from 1.0 (Critical Bear) to 5.0 (High Conviction Bull).
